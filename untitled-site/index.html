FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question about working with us? Here are the ones we hear most.

What areas do you cover?
We work throughout Romford and the surrounding Essex area. Get in touch with your postcode and we'll confirm we can help.
How much will my project cost?
Every garden is different, so we price after a free on-site consultation rather than guessing over the phone. Request a quote and we'll get back to you with a clear, no-obligation estimate.
How long does a typical project take?
Smaller jobs like fencing or turf can wrap up in a few days, while larger patio or full-garden transformations usually run one to three weeks. We'll give you a firm timeline before work starts.
Are you insured and guaranteed?
Yes — we're fully insured, and every project is backed by a workmanship warranty so you're covered long after we've finished.
Do I need to be home during the work?
No, as long as we have access to the outdoor space. Many of our clients are at work during the day — we'll keep you updated with photos as the project progresses.
Do you offer any warranty on your work?
Yes — every project is backed by a workmanship warranty, and we'll always come back to put things right if something isn't up to standard.
Can you help with garden design as well as the build?
Absolutely. Many clients come to us with just an idea of what they want — we'll help design the space, then carry out the full build ourselves.
What forms of payment do you accept?
We accept bank transfer and all major debit and credit cards. For larger projects, we can also agree a staged payment schedule tied to project milestones.
